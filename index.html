User Guide
1. Download Video / Audio
  • Paste Link/ID or click 'Find Video' / press Shift+T to pick a video directly from page.
  • Select desired quality or choose Download Type (Video, Audio, Media).
  • Click 'Run yt-dlp (.bat)' to auto-download or 'Copy CMD' for manual run.
2. Download Playlist / Mix
  • Switch mode to 'Playlist' and paste the playlist URL.
  • Select the videos you want to download from the list.
  • Click 'Run yt-dlp (.bat)' to batch download all selected items.
3. Export Metadata
  • Check/uncheck desired metadata fields in the checklist.
  • Enter a filename and click '.json' or '.csv' to export.
4. System Requirements

Make sure yt-dlp and ffmpeg are installed and added to your system PATH to execute .bat scripts.
